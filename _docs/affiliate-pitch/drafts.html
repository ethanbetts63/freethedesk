Podcast partner pitchthree drafts.

Three ways of putting the same offer to a podcast host. We send each to an even share of the list and keep the one that gets the most replies. Drafted 24 October 2026.

The same in all three

  • A free report on their business. The same report customers pay $499 for.
  • Half of every report their listeners buy: $250 a report, paid out through Stripe.
  • A code for their listeners, worth {discount} off.
  • One next step: reply with your website.
  • The footer: an unsubscribe link, our postal address, and where we found their email (their podcast feed).

Still to settle

  • Does the $250 repeat? These drafts follow "every report sold", so a listener on monthly reports pays the host every month. If it is the first report only, versions B and C change one line each.
  • The listener discount. Shown as {discount}. $50 was the suggestion.
  • The sending address and the postal address for the footer: the marketing email from the side note.

{yellow} marks a field filled per podcast. The report images are the live sample report at freethedesk.com.au/seo/sample-report.
