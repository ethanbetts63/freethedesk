of `/seo` and `/website-development`. The current page (built 2026-11-15, then cut
to half its words) has one card, one section and seven questions. The rivals here
were chosen by where they rank, not from lists. Each search was read once on
google.com.au with `pws=0`, from Perth, in the analytics@ Chrome. The Melbourne,
Sydney and Brisbane searches were also run from Perth, so read them as a guide.
The pages were fetched the same day (30 directly, 3 in Chrome).

## What the results say

- **One page can rank for both searches.** Mycelium is #3 for the GBP search and
  #2 for "local SEO Perth". Its title covers both: "Local SEO Perth | Google Maps
  and Business Profile Optimisation". No other service page is in both Perth top nines.
- **For "local SEO", a domain that matches the search ranks first** in Perth and
  Melbourne. localseomelbourne.com.au does it with 251 words. The same thing
  happened for AI SEO (`service-page-research.md`).
- **The Perth bar is low.** None of the seven Perth service pages above
  publishes a price. Distl (#1) has about 950 words, no prices, no proof and two
  questions. Lollipop (#5) has 500 words. PWD (#4) is the only heavy one: 3,500
  words, case studies with numbers, a 4.8 review rating and ten questions.

## What they have in common

- **An H1 that is the search,** word for word or nearly ("Google Business Profile
  Optimisation Brisbane").
- **The map pack,** shown as three places to win.
- **A list of what gets done.** Of the 19 GBP pages, 17 mention categories, 17
  posts, 16 photos and 15 the description. About 12 mention competitor analysis,
  11 citations or NAP, 11 getting more reviews and 11 suspension or verification.
  Nine mention review replies and nine reporting.
- **Steps,** usually four: audit, complete, activate, review (Balar's wording).
- **A free audit or check** to start.

## Where they differ

- **Prices shown or hidden.** See the table above.
- **One-off, monthly, or both.** Panda, Seedwell, Local Rank and EZY Boost sell
  a fix plus an optional monthly plan, side by side.
- **Saying what they won't do.** Mycelium has "What we will not do". Smart Local
  has "What can be promised, and what cannot". Upsoul has "We won't promise you
  rankings". Barrell has "Your Google profile stays yours". The current page says
  these things only in its questions.
- **Proof.** PWD, Digital Hitmen and Local Digital show case studies with
  numbers. Most cheap one-off pages show none.
- **A niche.** Gleemium's H1 names plumbing, HVAC and roofing.
- **Rank by suburb.** Local Digital has "Sydney is dozens of map packs" and Smart
  Local has "Why you show up in one suburb and vanish in the next". No GBP page
  shows a map grid of where a business ranks (0 of 19).
- **Tracking.** Only one of the 19 GBP pages mentions a tracked (UTM) website
  link. Panda's one-off includes UTM tracking and a handover guide.

## Where freethedesk can differ

- **A Perth price on the page.** No Perth GBP rival publishes one.
- **Two names in the title:** "local SEO" for the volume, and Google Business
  Profile (and Google My Business) for the search the page answers. Mycelium
  shows one page can rank for both.
- **Measured results.** The tracked website link, and the profile's own
  performance figures (calls, direction requests, website clicks) read before
  and after the work. Almost no rival offers this.
- **A map of where you rank.** A before-and-after grid would show the "you vanish
  one suburb over" point. No GBP rival shows one.
- **What we won't do,** given its own section, not left in the questions.
